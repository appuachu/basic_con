🔑 UDP Key Features

🚀

Connectionless

No handshake — data sent immediately without establishing connection

⚡

Fast

Minimal overhead makes UDP faster than TCP

📦

Lightweight

Only 8-byte header vs TCP's 20+ bytes

❌

No Guarantee

Packets may be lost, duplicated, or arrive out of order

📡

Broadcast/Multicast

Supports one-to-many communication

🎮

Real-time

Ideal for gaming, streaming, VoIP where speed matters
