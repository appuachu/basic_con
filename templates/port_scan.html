🎯 What Is Port Scanning?

    

        

            

                
🏢

                
The Building

                
Each IP address is like a building. It has 65,535 "doors" (ports).

            

            
➡️

            

                
🚪

                
Knocking Doors

                
Port scanning is like knocking on each door to see which ones open.

            

            
➡️

            

                
🔓

                
Finding Entry

                
Open ports reveal running services — and potential entry points.

            

        

    







    
💼 Why Do We Scan Ports?

    

        
🛡️
Security Auditing
Find open ports that shouldn't be exposed

        
🔍
Reconnaissance
Ethical hackers map target systems

        
📊
Asset Inventory
Track what services are running

        
🐛
Troubleshooting
Verify services are listening

        
⚠️
Attack Simulation
Red teams test defenses

        
✅
Compliance
Meet PCI-DSS, HIPAA, ISO 27001

Legal & Ethical Notice

            
Port scanning systems you don't own or don't have written permission to test is illegal in most countries.

            
    
                
  • ✅ Scan only your own systems
    • 
                
  • ✅ Get written authorization before testing client networks
    • 
                
  • ✅ Use scanning responsibly for security research
    • 
                
  • ❌ Never scan public IPs you don't own
    • 
                
  • ❌ Never use port scanning to attack or harass
