🎯 What Is Wireshark?

Wireshark is the world's foremost network protocol analyzer. It captures packets traveling across your network in real-time, decodes them layer by layer, and lets you filter, analyze, and extract everything from HTTP requests to VoIP audio. It's used by network engineers, security analysts, and forensic investigators worldwide.
